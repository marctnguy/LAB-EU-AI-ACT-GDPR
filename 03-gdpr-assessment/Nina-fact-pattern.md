A mid-size company uses an AI tool to screen job applications before a human
recruiter sees them. Candidates upload a resume and cover letter through a
careers page; the AI reads each document, extracts personal details (name,
contact info, education, work history, sometimes age or nationality if
mentioned), and produces a ranked shortlist with a fit score for each
candidate. Only the top-ranked candidates are shown to the human recruiter
by default; lower-ranked applicants are not actively reviewed unless the
recruiter manually expands the list. The system is hosted on a US-based
cloud vendor, and resumes are stored indefinitely in the company's
applicant-tracking database for future openings.
