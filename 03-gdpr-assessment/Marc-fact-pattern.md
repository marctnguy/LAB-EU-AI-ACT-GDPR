NordSecure Health, a German private health insurer headquartered in Hamburg, is expanding its individual health insurance business across Germany, Denmark, Sweden, Finland, and the Netherlands. To support this growth, the company plans to introduce an AI-assisted underwriting system that helps evaluate new insurance applications and provide consistent risk assessments across all markets.

When a customer applies for a policy, the system processes personal information such as name, date of birth, address, contact details, previous insurance history, medical questionnaires, declared medical conditions, claims history, and lifestyle information including smoking habits, alcohol consumption, BMI, and physical activity. Based on these inputs, the AI generates a risk score and recommends whether the application should be accepted, declined, or offered with adjusted premiums.

The applicants are residents of EU Member States, meaning the company processes personal data—including special category health data—from individuals located across the European Economic Area. The underwriting platform is hosted on a cloud infrastructure provided by a US-based technology vendor, which processes data on behalf of NordSecure Health. As a result, personal data may be subject to international transfers outside the EEA, requiring appropriate GDPR transfer safeguards. Health data is subject to enhanced protection under the GDPR and generally requires an additional legal basis beyond the standard processing conditions.

The AI does not make the final underwriting decision on its own. Instead, it provides a recommendation and supporting risk indicators to insurance underwriters, who review each application before issuing the final decision. In practice, underwriters usually follow the recommendation unless additional medical evidence or exceptional circumstances justify a different outcome. The company's objective is to shorten application processing times, improve consistency between underwriting teams, and identify potentially fraudulent applications while maintaining compliance with European data protection and AI regulations.
