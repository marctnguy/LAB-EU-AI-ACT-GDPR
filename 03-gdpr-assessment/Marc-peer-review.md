## Client Feedback

The recommendation is clear and provides a practical roadmap for moving the project forward. Before implementation, I would like additional clarification on how "meaningful human review" should operate in practice, particularly how underwriters should document decisions when they disagree with the AI's recommendation. I would also appreciate an explicit statement confirming whether the AI Act introduces any additional obligations beyond those already discussed under GDPR.
