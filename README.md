# EU AI Act & GDPR Consulting Engagement

This repository contains two related consulting labs completed as part of the AI Consulting Bootcamp. The work follows the same fictional client scenarios through two regulatory lenses: first under the EU AI Act, then under the GDPR.

## Repository structure

### 01-scenario-design
Creation of the hidden client scenarios used during the AI Act consulting exercise.

- Marc-client-briefs.md – Client scenarios shared with partner.
- Marc-private-answer-key.md – Hidden AI Act classifications and rationale.
- Nina-client-briefs.md – Client scenarios received from partner.

### 02-ai-act-assessment
First-pass EU AI Act compliance assessment of the client scenarios.

- Marc-approval-pack.md – AI Act assessment of Nina's scenarios.
- Nina-approval-pack.md – AI Act assessment of Marc's scenarios.

### 03-gdpr-assessment
GDPR audit of the selected scenarios from the AI Act exercise.

- Marc-fact-pattern.md – Fact pattern developed from Marc's AI Act scenario.
- Nina-fact-pattern.md – Fact pattern received from Nina and used for the GDPR audit.
- Marc-gdpr-audit-worksheet.md – GDPR audit worksheet.
- Marc-client-recommendation-memo.md – Final GDPR recommendation memo.
- Nina-peer-review.md – Feedback received from partner.

> This repository represents a consulting exercise and does not constitute legal advice.
